# Business Model

## Purpose

Describe the present operating model accurately without representing provider-hosted notarization as an Avenseal-operated platform.

**Status:** Current working standard
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-08

## Current customer flow

1. A customer discovers Avenseal and reviews service information.
2. The customer requests or schedules an appointment and pays through Avenseal when payment is required.
3. Avenseal provides appointment communications and Client Workspace access.
4. Identity verification and the remote session are completed through the online notarization provider.
5. A commissioned notary completes the notarization.

## Boundaries

Avenseal must not claim to operate the provider-hosted identity-verification or video-session platform. Whether a notarization can proceed remains subject to applicable law, document requirements, and professional review.

## To Be Finalized

Service catalog, pricing policy, cancellation policy, and partner economics need approved source material before being stated as final.
