# Competitive Position

## Purpose

Guide factual, differentiated market language.

**Status:** Current working standard
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-08

## Position

Avenseal offers a professional appointment experience for Florida Remote Online Notary customers: clear service information, scheduling, payment, communications, and a customer workspace, followed by a provider-hosted remote notarization session with a commissioned notary.

## Approved themes

- Florida Remote Online Notary
- Remote Online Notary (RON)
- Commissioned Florida Notary
- Secure identity verification through the online notarization provider
- Professional appointment experience

## Phrases to avoid

Do not claim same-day, instant, 24/7, guaranteed, fastest, fully automated, or provider ownership. Do not make broad legal or compliance claims without approved substantiation.
