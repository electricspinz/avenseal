# Mission, Vision, and Values

## Purpose

Keep Avenseal decisions and public language aligned with an explicit foundation.

**Status:** Current working standard
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-08

## Mission

Simplify secure Remote Online Notarization by providing a professional, trusted, and seamless scheduling experience.

## Vision

Become the trusted customer-facing platform for Florida Remote Online Notary services.

## Values

| Value | Operating meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Trust | Be precise about Avenseal's role and protect customer workflows. |
| Clarity | Explain next steps, pricing, and ownership without hype. |
| Professionalism | Use calm, reliable communication and deliberate operations. |
| Respect | Make the experience accessible and considerate of customer time. |
| Accountability | Keep important business actions reviewable and owned. |
