# Company Launch Checklist

**Status:** Internal launch control. A checked item requires dated evidence and an accountable owner outside this repository.

## Company and legal

- [ ] Avenseal LLC business details, address, and support inbox verified.
- [ ] Attorney-reviewed Privacy Policy and Terms approved for publication.
- [ ] Florida RON compliance, commissioned-notary process, and required disclosures approved.
- [ ] Retention, deletion, incident, and customer-support policies approved.

## Operational launch

- [ ] Owner/admin access and production organization configuration verified.
- [ ] BlueNotary operating process approved; no unverified API capability is enabled.
- [ ] Stripe live account, webhook, refund, receipt, and reconciliation process verified.
- [ ] Calendar, SMTP, and protected schedulers configured and exercised with safe staging evidence.
- [ ] Document scanner vendor approval and [staging acceptance](../03-product/document-scanner-staging-acceptance.md) complete.
- [ ] Monitoring, alert ownership, backups, domain/SSL, and incident contacts verified.

## Customer-facing launch

- [ ] Final legal copy is published separately through an approved production change.
- [ ] Pricing reflects only the disclosed notarial fee.
- [ ] Customer communications identify Avenseal’s coordination role and the notary/BlueNotary handoff.

See the [Production Readiness Audit](../03-product/document-scanner-staging-acceptance.md) for current launch gates and the [attorney checklist](../02-legal/attorney-review-checklist.md) for legal review.
