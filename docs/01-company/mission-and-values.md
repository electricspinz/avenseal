# Mission and Values

## Mission

Make online notarization scheduling and preparation clear, safe, and dependable while preserving the commissioned notary’s independent authority.

## Positioning promise

**The easiest and most trusted way to get a document notarized online.**

This is a service promise, not a claim that Avenseal performs the notarization or guarantees an outcome.

## Operating values

| Value | Applied behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Clear boundaries | Say when BlueNotary or the commissioned notary owns a step. |
| Privacy by default | Collect only what is needed; do not expose links, tokens, documents, or provider details. |
| Honest operations | Use verified status and safe failure states; never imply a session, payment, or approval exists without evidence. |
| Human authority | Notarial eligibility, identity proofing, and refusal decisions remain with the commissioned notary. |
| Recovery over concealment | Preserve quarantine, audit, and queue evidence; communicate safe next steps. |

Related product principles are maintained in the [vision](../00-overview/vision.md) and [content style guide](../01-design-system/content-style.md).
