# Public Launch Business Information Checklist

This internal checklist identifies information that needs owner approval before Avenseal publishes final legal, contact, and local-business information.

| Information | Required public surfaces | Approval needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public business name and legal-entity display format | Footer, Contact, Privacy, Terms, Google Business Profile, structured data | Owner |
| Mailing address or approved service-area policy | Contact, Privacy, Terms, Google Business Profile, structured data | Owner |
| Monitored public support email | Contact, Footer, Google Business Profile | Owner |
| Public phone number, if any | Contact, Google Business Profile | Owner |
| Business hours and appointment availability policy | Contact, Google Business Profile | Owner |
| Service area | Contact, Google Business Profile, structured data | Owner |
| Final Privacy Policy | `/privacy`, footer link, booking consent | Legal and owner |
| Final Terms of Service | `/terms`, footer link, booking consent | Legal and owner |
| Approved refund, cancellation, and rescheduling policy | Pricing, Terms, appointment communications where applicable | Legal and owner |

## Publication gate

Do not index `/contact`, `/privacy`, or `/terms`, add LocalBusiness structured data, or create a Google Business Profile listing until the relevant approved information is available and consistent across every public surface.
