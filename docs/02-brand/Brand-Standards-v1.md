# Avenseal Brand Standards v1

## Purpose

Provide the working standard for consistent, accessible Avenseal expression.

**Status:** Current working standard
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-08

## Brand foundation

Avenseal exists to make secure Remote Online Notarization scheduling feel professional, trusted, and clear. The promise is **“Trust Every Signature.”** See [Mission, Vision, and Values](../01-company/Mission-Vision-Values.md).

## Personality, voice, and tone

Professional, clear, helpful, calm, modern, trustworthy, and never hype-driven. Lead with what a customer can do now; distinguish Avenseal's customer experience from the provider-hosted identity-verification and session platform.

## Visual system

The current working visual direction is deep navy, emerald, and mist/light-neutral backgrounds. Use a restrained legal-tech aesthetic: generous whitespace, border-led cards, subtle depth, readable hierarchy, and visible focus states. Exact color values, font families, and final assets are **To Be Finalized** unless approved assets or code establish them.

## Logo, type, imagery, and icons

Use approved logo variants only; preserve clear space and legibility. Typography must prioritize readable, high-contrast hierarchy. Photography should be calm and credible rather than staged or sensational. Illustration and iconography should be simple, supportive, and non-decorative.

## Website and marketing graphics

Keep calls to action clear, use factual trust language, and avoid unverified badges, seals, security claims, or certifications. Decorative graphics must not obscure content or accessibility.

## Accessibility

Maintain contrast, semantic headings, descriptive text alternatives, keyboard focus, and motion restraint. Never encode meaning by color alone.

## Approved messaging and phrases to avoid

Use the positioning in [Competitive Position](../01-company/Competitive-Position.md). Avoid “instant,” “24/7,” “guaranteed,” “fully automated,” and claims that Avenseal hosts the RON session.
