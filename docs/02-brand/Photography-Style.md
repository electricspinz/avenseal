# Photography Style

## Purpose

Guide credible, launch-appropriate imagery.

**Status:** Current working standard
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-08

## Direction

Favor calm, natural, professional imagery that supports appointment preparation, a commissioned notary, and secure remote-work context. Use inclusive, realistic composition and generous negative space for copy.

## Avoid

Avoid stock-photo clichés, exaggerated security visuals, courthouse imagery implying legal advice, exposed document contents, identity documents, screens with customer data, and imagery that implies Avenseal operates the provider-hosted session.
