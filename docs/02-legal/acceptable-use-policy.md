# Acceptable Use Policy Draft

**Attorney-review draft — not final legal advice and not approved for publication.**  
**Effective date:** `[Attorney to insert]`

Users may use Avenseal only for lawful appointment coordination. Users must not submit false information, impersonate another person, misuse a Client Workspace link, attempt to bypass payment or access controls, upload malware or prohibited material, interfere with services, or use the service for unlawful notarization-related activity.

Avenseal may pause a request for safety, fraud, security, legal, or notarial-review reasons. The commissioned notary remains responsible for notarial decisions. Counsel must complete enforcement, notice, suspension, and appeal terms before publication.
