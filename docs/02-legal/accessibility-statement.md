# Accessibility Statement Draft

**Attorney-review and accessibility-review draft — not approved for publication.**  
**Effective date:** `[Attorney to insert]`

Avenseal LLC intends to make Avenseal.com usable with keyboard navigation and assistive technology. The product design standards require semantic markup, visible focus, readable status text, and no color-only primary workflow. Accessibility feedback contact is appointments@avenseal.com; no public phone is offered at launch.

Before publication, accessibility specialists and counsel must define the conformance target, known limitations, alternative-access process, response timeframe, and review cadence.
