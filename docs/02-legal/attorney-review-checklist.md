# Attorney Review and Launch Checklist

**Internal checklist.** Each item needs a documented legal conclusion; unchecked items block publication or launch as applicable.

- [ ] Confirm Avenseal LLC legal entity, business address, website, and support email.
- [ ] Finalize Privacy Policy, Terms, Florida RON disclosures, refund policy, accessibility statement, and acceptable-use policy.
- [ ] Confirm age/minor policy, customer consent, recording/identity-related notices, and BlueNotary role disclosures.
- [ ] Approve data-processing, retention/deletion, security incident, legal-hold, and customer-rights handling.
- [ ] Confirm fees, refund timing, tax/consumer disclosures, and payment-provider terms.
- [ ] Review BlueNotary and document-scanner vendor contracts, DPA, subprocessors, residency, retention, and breach terms.
- [ ] Confirm required Florida and other applicable jurisdictional licenses, registrations, insurance, and recordkeeping.
- [ ] Approve final public publication change separately from these drafts.
