## Draft operating disclosures

- Self-service use is limited to people age 18 or older; matters involving minors require manual review.
- After payment, customers may provide temporary pre-session documents. Completed notarized documents are not intentionally retained by Avenseal.
- Pre-session uploads are targeted for deletion within seven days after completion/cancellation and ordinarily within 30 days for unresolved/rescheduled matters, subject to legal hold, law, fraud/security investigation, or documented customer request.
- A commissioned Florida online notary using BlueNotary performs the actual notarization and identity-related session work.
