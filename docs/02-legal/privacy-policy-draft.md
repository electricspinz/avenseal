# Privacy Policy

This is the Knowledge Base copy of the public Privacy Policy at `/privacy`. It is maintained to match the public policy in substance.

## 1. Introduction and scope

Avenseal LLC, doing business as Avenseal ("Avenseal," "we," "us," or "our"), provides customer-facing appointment, payment, preparation, communication, and customer-workspace services for Remote Online Notary appointments. This Policy covers information processed through avenseal.com, booking and customer interfaces, appointment communications, and related services.

This Policy does not govern the independent practices of an online notarization provider, payment processor, or other third party that processes information under its own terms or privacy notice.

## 2. Information we collect

- Information provided for appointments, payments, Client Workspace access, uploaded materials, and communications, including contact, appointment, document-related, message, note, and consent information.
- Appointment and transaction information, including service selection, requested schedule, appointment status, payment status, and communications.
- Security and operational information, including IP and request information used for security and rate limiting, browser/device information made available with web requests, timestamps, access logs, and use of token-protected customer features.

## 3. Documents and payments

Where available, the Client Workspace accepts appointment-related materials for document-preparation and review workflows, system protection, and appointment support. Private storage and security controls such as quarantine or scanning may be used where configured. Avenseal is not a public document archive and does not represent that it retains completed notarized documents.

Payments may be processed through Stripe Checkout or another disclosed processor. Payment-card information is submitted directly to the processor; Avenseal receives and retains transaction information needed to administer the payment, such as payment status, amount, currency, and processor references.

## 4. Identity verification and remote notarization providers

Avenseal does not host the identity-verification or remote audiovisual notarization environment. Identity verification, credential analysis, knowledge-based authentication where required, remote sessions, provider-required identity information, and provider records may be processed by the independent online notarization provider and commissioned notary under their own terms, privacy practices, and legal obligations.

## 5. Uses and disclosures

We use information to provide and improve the Services; coordinate appointments; process applicable payments; communicate with customers; provide Client Workspace and document workflows; maintain security, prevent fraud and misuse, troubleshoot, enforce agreements, comply with legal obligations, and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

We may share information with service providers for hosting, database, storage, communications, payment, security, and infrastructure; with the online notarization provider and commissioned notary as needed for an appointment and provider-hosted session; and with calendar providers when used. We may also disclose information to advisers, insurers, authorities, courts, or other parties when required or appropriate by law or to protect rights, safety, security, or the Services, and in a corporate transaction subject to applicable law.

## 6. Browser storage, retention, and security

The public booking experience may use browser local storage to preserve an in-progress booking draft. Necessary cookies may be used for authenticated administrative functions. We use Google Analytics 4 to measure aggregated use of public pages and the booking funnel. We do not send names, email addresses, phone numbers, document information, appointment notes, payment-card information, identity-verification information, or secure appointment-link tokens to Google Analytics.

We retain information as reasonably necessary to provide Services, maintain records, comply with law, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and protect security and integrity. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, but cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Rights, international users, children, and updates

Depending on residence and applicable law, a person may request access, correction, deletion, or information about personal information we process. We may verify a request before responding. Florida residents may contact us with privacy questions or requests; this Policy does not state that Avenseal is subject to every state-law threshold.

Avenseal is based in Florida. Information may be processed in the United States and other locations where service providers operate, subject to applicable law. The Services are not directed to children under 18 for self-service contracting, though an adult may arrange a notarization involving a minor where legally permissible. We may revise this Policy by posting the update and revising the Last Updated date.
