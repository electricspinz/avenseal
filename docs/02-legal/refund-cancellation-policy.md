# Refund & Cancellation Policy Draft

**Attorney-review draft — not final legal advice and not approved for publication.**  
**Effective date:** `[Attorney to insert]`

| Situation | Draft treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Cancellation before appointment | Full refund. |
| Rescheduling | Existing payment transfers without an additional fee. |
| First no-show | One courtesy reschedule. |
| Identity verification failure | Refund or one complimentary reschedule. |
| Notary refusal | Full refund. |
| Technical failure | Refund or complimentary reschedule. |
| Completed notarization | Nonrefundable except duplicate charge or billing error. |

Only the disclosed notarial fee is charged at launch. No separate platform, technology, or convenience fee applies. Counsel and finance operations must approve refund authority, timing, payment-provider workflow, recordkeeping, and customer notices before publication.
