These draft terms govern use of Avenseal LLC’s appointment and coordination services at Avenseal.com. Avenseal coordinates scheduling, payment, temporary pre-session document intake, communications, and Client Workspace access. A commissioned Florida online notary using BlueNotary performs the notarization. Avenseal does not provide legal advice or guarantee that a document can be notarized.

## Draft service terms

- Users must be 18 or older for self-service use. Requests involving minors require manual review.
- Customers must provide accurate booking and payment information and may not misuse secure links or upload prohibited material.
- Only the disclosed notarial fee applies at launch; no separate platform, technology, or convenience fee is charged.
- The [Refund & Cancellation Policy](refund-cancellation-policy.md) is incorporated after attorney approval.
- The commissioned notary retains independent discretion to refuse or defer notarization as required by law and professional duties.

Counsel must complete dispute, governing-law, arbitration/class-action, limitation-of-liability, consent, notice, accessibility, and state-specific terms before publication.
