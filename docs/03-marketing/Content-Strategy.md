# Content Strategy

## Purpose

Plan useful, accurate content that earns trust rather than traffic through unsupported claims.

**Status:** Planned / working strategy
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-08

## Content themes

- How Remote Online Notarization appointments work
- Preparing for a provider-hosted remote session
- Florida RON terminology and common questions
- Appointment, payment, and Client Workspace expectations

## Editorial controls

Every piece needs a factual owner, a review for role accuracy, and a clear distinction between information and legal advice. Avoid keyword stuffing, false urgency, and claims about provider capabilities not verified by approved material.
