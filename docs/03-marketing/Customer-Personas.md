# Customer Personas

## Purpose

Describe audience needs without treating assumptions as demographic facts.

**Status:** Planned / working strategy
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-08

## Primary needs

| Audience situation | Need Avenseal should address |
| --- | --- |
| Individual with a document requiring RON | Clear eligibility, appointment, payment, and preparation steps |
| Remote or traveling client | Plain explanation of a provider-hosted session and applicable requirements |
| Busy professional | Predictable scheduling and concise communications |
| Referral source | Accurate service description and dependable appointment handoff |

## Guardrails

Clients may be located worldwide only subject to applicable law and document requirements. Do not promise eligibility, acceptance, or completion before required review.
