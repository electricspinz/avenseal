# Marketing Launch Checklist

## Purpose

Coordinate public marketing readiness without substituting for legal, operational, or technical launch approval.

**Status:** Planned / working checklist
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-08

## Before public promotion

- [ ] Confirm approved business contact information and service-area wording.
- [ ] Confirm public-site copy accurately describes the provider-hosted session.
- [ ] Confirm final logo, social, and Google Business assets.
- [ ] Confirm legal drafts and public disclosures have required approval.
- [ ] Review metadata, canonical URLs, and indexability.
- [ ] Confirm Google Business Profile categories, hours, and service-area settings.
- [ ] Review booking, payment, and communication paths with synthetic data.
- [ ] Establish owner for reviews and inbound inquiries.
