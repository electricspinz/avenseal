# Marketing Strategy

## Purpose

Set a factual launch marketing direction for Avenseal.

**Status:** Planned / working strategy
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-08

## Objective

Help qualified customers understand Avenseal's service, schedule confidently, and arrive prepared for a provider-hosted remote notarization session with a commissioned notary.

## Current foundation

The public site, appointment request/scheduling experience, payment, communications, and Client Workspace are product surfaces. Marketing must describe them accurately and must not claim that Avenseal owns the identity-verification or remote-session platform.

## Channels

Prioritize the public website, Google Business Profile, helpful educational content, service-area discovery, and post-appointment review requests where appropriate. Channel budgets and campaign calendar are **To Be Finalized**.

## Success criteria

Track qualified inquiries, completed appointments, customer understanding, and review quality using approved, privacy-conscious reporting. Exact KPI targets are **To Be Finalized**.
