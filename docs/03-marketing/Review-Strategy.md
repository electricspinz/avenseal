# Review Strategy

## Purpose

Create a respectful, policy-aware approach to requesting and responding to customer reviews.

**Status:** Planned / working strategy
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-08

## Principles

Request reviews only after an appropriate completed customer interaction, make requests optional, and never condition service, refunds, or support on a favorable review. Do not expose appointment or document details in public replies.

## Operating approach

Use approved communications, monitor Google Business Profile reviews on an assigned cadence, and route sensitive concerns to private support. Incentives, templates, response authority, and escalation timing are **To Be Finalized**.
