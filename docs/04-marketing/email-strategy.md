# Email Strategy

**Status:** Content and lifecycle plan only. This does not create or enable automation.

| Message | Purpose | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Welcome | Explain next step after opted-in contact | Use approved consent/marketing basis. |
| Appointment reminders | Preparation and timing | Use existing operational communications only. |
| Educational follow-up | Share approved preparation content | Do not send legal advice. |
| Referral request | Request honest introductions where appropriate | No unapproved incentive. |
| Review request | Invite honest feedback after eligible completion | No positive-review condition. |
| Seasonal campaign | Share approved, relevant education | Review consent, relevance, and frequency. |

Use the existing [communications execution](../03-product/communications-execution.md) and [email template principles](../06-operations/email-templates.md). Marketing automation, consent model, unsubscribe handling, and campaign execution require separate legal and product approval.
