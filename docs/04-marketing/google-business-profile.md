## Assets and content

- Needed photos: approved brand mark, business/location image if accurate, secure professional workspace imagery, and approved educational graphics. Never use customer documents, IDs, session screenshots, or staged notarial claims.
- Q&A: answer scheduling, preparation, payment, rescheduling, and support questions with approved wording. Route legal/identity/notarial questions to the commissioned-notary process.
- Posts: one educational or availability-safe post each week; no unverified urgency claims.

## Reviews and maintenance

Request honest reviews after an eligible completed customer interaction. Do not condition service or compensation on review sentiment. Follow [Review Strategy](review-strategy.md).

Monthly: verify business facts, links, categories, photos, questions, review responses, duplicate listings, and access ownership. Keep account recovery information in approved operations storage, never this repository.
