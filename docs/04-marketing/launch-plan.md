# 30-Day Organic Launch Plan

**No paid advertising.** Start only after legal/public pages, production operations, and launch controls are approved.

| Week | Objective | Actions | Evidence / KPI |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Establish accurate foundation | Verify website, Google Business Profile, service facts, final legal links, analytics ownership, and support inbox. | Approved facts; indexed pages; support response baseline. |
| 2 | Publish core education | Publish approved Tier 1 pages and initial FAQ/preparation content. | Page quality review; organic impressions. |
| 3 | Begin relationships | Introduce approved referral materials to a small set of relevant partners; publish educational social content. | Qualified conversations; no unapproved compensation. |
| 4 | Improve from evidence | Review support themes, booking funnel evidence, feedback, and content gaps. | Provisional conversion, response-time, and review measures. |

Weekly operating review: confirm claims remain accurate, no legal/identity/notarial boundary is misstated, and no customer data appears in marketing content. See [SEO Roadmap](seo-roadmap.md), [Review Strategy](review-strategy.md), and [Launch Checklist](../01-company/launch-checklist.md).
