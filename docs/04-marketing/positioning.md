# Brand Positioning

## Position

**The easiest and most trusted way to get a document notarized online.**

Avenseal helps customers schedule, pay for, prepare for, and access an online notarization appointment. BlueNotary hosts the RON platform, and a commissioned Florida online notary performs the notarial act.

## Messaging guardrails

- Say “schedule an online notarization appointment,” not “Avenseal notarizes your document.”
- Do not guarantee identity verification, acceptance, completion, timing, or legal validity.
- State fees clearly: the disclosed notarial fee only at launch.
- Do not use unverifiable claims such as “instant,” “best,” “guaranteed,” or “nationwide legal advice.”

## Proof points to verify before use

Use only current, documented evidence: clear scheduling, secure Client Workspace access, transparent preparation guidance, payment coordination, and a commissioned-notary BlueNotary handoff. Legal and operational claims require approval through the [launch checklist](../01-company/launch-checklist.md).
