# Referral Partner Strategy

## Initial audience

Potential referral relationships may include attorneys, title/real-estate professionals, financial planners, elder-care coordinators, and businesses that need lawful notarization coordination. This is relationship development, not a paid lead program.

## Partner proposition

Avenseal provides a clear scheduling and preparation path, transparent disclosed fee, customer communications, and a commissioned-notary BlueNotary handoff. Partners must not promise a notarization outcome or provide legal advice through Avenseal materials.

## Review generation principles

- Request honest feedback only after a completed service interaction.
- Never condition service, refunds, or incentives on a positive review.
- Never create, edit, or publish fabricated reviews.
- Protect customer privacy; do not disclose document, identity, or session information.
