# Google Review Strategy

## When and how to ask

Ask only after an eligible completed service interaction and after any immediate support issue is resolved. Do not ask after a refusal, unresolved complaint, refund dispute, or security incident. Never offer incentives for positive reviews.

## Approved wording

**Email:** “Thank you for choosing Avenseal. If you would like to share honest feedback about your scheduling and appointment experience, you may leave a review at the approved link. Your feedback helps us improve.”

**SMS:** `[Placeholder — obtain consent, legal review, and approved messaging process before use.]`

## Response standards

- Thank positive reviewers without discussing appointment, document, identity, payment, or session details.
- For negative feedback, acknowledge the concern, invite a private response through appointments@avenseal.com, and do not debate facts publicly.
- Escalate legal, privacy, security, refund, or notarial issues before responding.

## KPIs

Track verified review count, average rating, response time, unresolved-review rate, and review-request rate. Targets are provisional; see [KPIs](../07-finance/kpis.md).
