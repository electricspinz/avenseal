# Local and Florida RON SEO Roadmap

**Status:** Initial content foundation; no paid-campaign plan.

## Priorities

1. Maintain accurate public pages for online notarization appointment scheduling, pricing, FAQ, privacy, and terms after legal approval.
2. Create attorney-reviewed educational content about preparing for an online notarization appointment, acceptable preparation steps, scheduling, payment, and the BlueNotary handoff.
3. Build location-relevant pages only where claims, availability, and jurisdictional language are reviewed and accurate.

## Educational topics

- How an online notarization appointment is prepared.
- What happens before a BlueNotary session.
- How to reschedule or request support.
- Document-preparation reminders without legal advice.
- What customers should expect if a notary cannot proceed.

## Review principles

Never publish legal conclusions, unverified availability, fabricated reviews, provider capability claims, or claims that Avenseal performs identity proofing/notarization. Final legal pages are a prerequisite for launch SEO work.
