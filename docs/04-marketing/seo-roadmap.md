# SEO Roadmap

**Status:** Internal, post-launch organic-growth plan. No paid advertising, legal claims, or availability guarantees.

## Search strategy

Target customers seeking an online notarization appointment in Florida first, then build national education only where service eligibility and disclosures are reviewed. Position around trust, simplicity, professionalism, speed, and customer experience—not the lowest price.

## Keyword clusters and priority

| Tier | Intent | Clusters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 | High buying intent | Florida Online Notary; Remote Online Notary; Online Notary Florida; Same Day Online Notary; National Online Notary; General Notary Services |
| Tier 2 | Educational/preparation | Estate Planning Notary; Power of Attorney Notary; Affidavit Notary; Real Estate Notary; Business Notary; Medical Notary; witness and document-preparation questions |
| Tier 3 | Local authority | Florida RON education, Florida location/service-area pages, attorney-reviewed FAQs, local partner education |

“Same day” must describe only an approved scheduling option; it must never guarantee immediate availability or notarization. “National” must not imply unreviewed jurisdictional coverage.

## Service-page roadmap

Create a page only after legal, service-eligibility, and customer-support wording are reviewed. Each page uses a single appointment CTA and links to Pricing, FAQ, preparation, and applicable educational content.

Pages must not give legal advice, determine document eligibility, or claim that Avenseal performs identity proofing or notarization. The commissioned Florida online notary uses BlueNotary for the RON session.

## Operating cadence

1. Publish approved Tier 1 pages and final legal pages.
2. Add reviewed FAQ and Tier 2 education based on recurring support questions.
3. Add Tier 3 authority content only after Florida wording and service facts are attorney-reviewed.
4. Monthly: check page accuracy, broken links, CTA flow, search impressions, and support-feedback themes.
