# Social Media Strategy

## Launch channels

| Platform | Purpose | Cadence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook | Local education and approved updates | 2 posts/week |
| Instagram | Simple preparation education and brand trust | 2 posts/week |
| LinkedIn | Referral-partner and professional education | 1 post/week |
| Google Business | Approved educational/service updates | 1 post/week |
| YouTube | Future consideration after an approved production process | Not at launch |

TikTok is not recommended at launch.

## Content mix

Use approximately 80% educational/support content and 20% promotional content. Educational posts cover preparation, scheduling, privacy, rescheduling, and the difference between Avenseal coordination and the commissioned-notary/BlueNotary session. Promotional posts use approved booking CTAs only.

Never publish customer data, document images, identity material, session links, legal advice, unverified turnaround promises, or claims that Avenseal hosts the RON session.
