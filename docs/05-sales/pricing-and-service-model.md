# Pricing and Service Model

## Launch model

Avenseal initially offers direct-to-customer online notary appointment coordination in the United States. The customer pays only the disclosed notarial fee at launch. There is no separate platform, technology, coordination, or convenience fee.

The service includes scheduling, payment coordination, temporary pre-session document intake after payment, communications, and Client Workspace access. The commissioned Florida online notary performs the actual notarization through BlueNotary.

Refund and cancellation handling follows the attorney-review [policy draft](../02-legal/refund-cancellation-policy.md) until a final approved policy is published.

## Later-phase concept

Licensing the scheduling, intake, payment, and communication platform to other notaries is a later-phase concept. It is not a current launch feature, partner offer, or customer promise.
