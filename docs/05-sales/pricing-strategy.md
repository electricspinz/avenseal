# Pricing Strategy

## Launch position

Avenseal charges only the disclosed notarial fee at launch. There is no technology, platform, convenience, coordination, or hidden fee. The pricing experience should be clear before the customer proceeds to payment.

## Why this approach

Compete on trust, simplicity, professionalism, speed, and customer experience—not lowest price. A simple disclosed-fee model reduces customer confusion and aligns the coordination role with the commissioned notary’s independent authority.

## Future consideration

Premium offerings, additional coordination services, business plans, and platform licensing are **future considerations**, not launch features or promises. Each would require legal, pricing, product, payment, and operational approval before communication or implementation.
