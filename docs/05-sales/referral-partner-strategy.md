# Referral Partner Strategy

## Priority partner categories

Estate planning attorneys, real estate agents, mortgage brokers, title companies, insurance agencies, financial advisors, CPAs, HR departments, hospitals, assisted-living organizations, schools, and small businesses may refer customers who need appointment coordination.

## Ideal referral process

1. Confirm the partner’s client need fits an approved appointment-coordination use case.
2. Explain Avenseal’s role: scheduling, payment, secure pre-session upload, Client Workspace, and communications.
3. Explain that a commissioned Florida online notary performs notarization using BlueNotary.
4. Provide only approved public booking/support materials; do not collect client documents or identity information from the partner.
5. Record relationship activity in approved business systems, not customer appointment notes.
6. Review client experience and support themes without exchanging confidential appointment/session details.

This is not a paid-referral program. Any compensation, endorsement, privacy, or regulated-profession arrangement requires legal/business approval before use.
