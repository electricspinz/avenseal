# SEO Strategy

## Purpose

Guide sustainable organic discovery through accurate public content.

**Status:** Planned / working strategy
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-08

## Objective

Help searchers who need Florida Remote Online Notary information find clear, accurate service and appointment guidance.

## Current approach

Use useful public pages, distinct metadata, canonical URLs, crawl controls, sitemap coverage, and natural language. Core terms may include Florida Remote Online Notary, Remote Online Notary, Online Notary, Florida Notary, RON, and Commissioned Florida Notary.

## Guardrails

Do not keyword-stuff, create thin location pages, or claim instant, 24/7, guaranteed, or provider-owned capabilities. Content must accurately distinguish Avenseal from the provider-hosted identity-verification and remote session.

## To Be Finalized

Search Console ownership, keyword research, technical crawl baseline, and reporting cadence.
