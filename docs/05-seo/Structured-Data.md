# Structured Data

## Purpose

Record safe structured-data policy for future approved implementation.

**Status:** To Be Finalized
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-08

## Current position

Do not publish schema markup that asserts unapproved legal, location, rating, service, availability, or compliance facts. The repository's public metadata implementation should be reviewed before any schema is introduced.

## Planned approach

After business details are approved, consider narrowly factual Organization or LocalBusiness-compatible markup only if it accurately reflects a service-area business and does not imply a public storefront. Review official search-engine guidance before publishing.

## Required approval inputs

Legal business name, approved contact details, service area, public address policy, social profiles, logo assets, and review policy.
