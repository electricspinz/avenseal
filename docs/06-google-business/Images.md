# Google Business Images

## Purpose

Prepare a factual visual-asset plan for the Google Business Profile.

**Status:** To Be Finalized
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-08

## Required assets

Use the approved Google Business Profile Logo and Cover listed in [Brand Assets](../02-brand/Brand-Assets.md). Supporting images should be professionally licensed, representative, accessible, and free of customer documents, credentials, or provider dashboard content.

## Avoid

Do not use seals, certifications, office/storefront imagery, or security imagery that cannot be substantiated. Do not imply Avenseal operates the provider-hosted session.
