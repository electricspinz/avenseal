# Google Business Posts

## Purpose

Set safe post guidelines for launch and ongoing profile management.

**Status:** Planned / working strategy
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-08

## Post structure

Use a clear headline, one factual customer benefit, one approved action, and a destination on the public site. Suggested topics: appointment preparation, how the provider-hosted session works, and clear service explanations.

## Review gate

Posts require factual review and must avoid price claims, legal advice, time-sensitive availability promises, and unsupported provider or compliance claims.
