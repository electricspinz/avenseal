# Google Business Reviews

## Purpose

Define an ethical operating approach for reviews.

**Status:** Planned / working strategy
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-08

## Current guidance

Ask only at an appropriate time after a customer interaction. Participation is optional. Never require a positive review, offer unapproved incentives, or disclose appointment/document details in a response.

## Response principles

Thank the reviewer, remain concise, and move sensitive matters to private support. Do not confirm customer identity, appointment status, or notarization details.
