# Daily Opening and Closing Checklists

## Opening

- [ ] Review appointment schedule, payment/readiness blockers, and safe communications status.
- [ ] Confirm authorized staff access and configured operational integrations.
- [ ] Review failed queue items and escalation ownership.
- [ ] Confirm no unresolved security incident requires containment.

## Closing

- [ ] Review completed, cancelled, rescheduled, and failed appointments.
- [ ] Confirm customer communications reached safe terminal states.
- [ ] Review temporary-upload retention actions due under approved policy.
- [ ] Record unresolved issues with safe references and assigned owner.

Use aggregate operational views only; do not export private documents, tokens, session links, or payment data into checklists.
