# Operations Manual

## Customer journey and ownership

| Stage | Avenseal | BlueNotary | Commissioned notary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Booking | Scheduling, payment coordination, communication | None | Reviews requests as needed |
| Preparation | Client Workspace, temporary document intake after payment | None | Determines notarial readiness where applicable |
| Live session | Handoff and support coordination | Hosts identity/session platform | Performs identity/notarial work and decides whether to proceed |
| Completion | Customer communication, retention workflow | Provider-side session artifacts | Performs/records notarial act as required |

## Escalation rules

Escalate security incidents immediately under [SOP: Security Incident](sop-security-incident.md). Escalate identity, eligibility, refusal, or notarial questions to the commissioned notary; do not decide them in Avenseal. Escalate payment/refund exceptions to the authorized finance operator.

Use the [daily checklists](daily-checklists.md) and the product [readiness domain](../03-product/appointment-readiness.md) as operational evidence.
