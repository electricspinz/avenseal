# SOP: After Appointment

1. Confirm only supported status evidence; do not claim notarization completion without provider/notary confirmation.
2. Send approved follow-up communication when eligible.
3. Do not intentionally retain completed notarized documents in Avenseal.
4. Start the approved temporary-upload retention/deletion process after completion or cancellation.
5. Escalate document delivery questions to the commissioned notary/BlueNotary process.
