# SOP: Before Appointment

1. Confirm payment and appointment status in Avenseal.
2. Confirm required preparation and document-readiness facts without making identity/notarial determinations.
3. Confirm the external session is eligible before exposing its launch link.
4. Send only approved customer communication templates.
5. Escalate a missing/invalid session, payment concern, or readiness blocker to the responsible operator or commissioned notary.

BlueNotary is responsible for its platform session; Avenseal does not host the RON meeting.
