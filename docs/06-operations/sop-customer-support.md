# SOP: Customer Support

Use appointments@avenseal.com. No public phone is offered at launch.

- Verify only the minimum information needed to locate an appointment through approved tools.
- Send secure access or status links only through existing controlled workflows.
- Do not request government ID numbers, documents by ordinary email, passwords, or session credentials.
- Explain Avenseal’s coordination role and route identity/notarial questions to the commissioned notary.
- Escalate access, payment, document, security, and legal requests through the appropriate SOP.
