# SOP: During Appointment

1. Greet the customer as an Avenseal scheduling/support contact.
2. Direct the customer to the authorized BlueNotary handoff when eligible.
3. Do not perform identity proofing, credential analysis, or notarial decisions.
4. If the session fails, record only safe operational facts and follow [Failed Appointment SOP](sop-failed-appointment.md).
5. Protect session URLs, tokens, documents, and payment data from chat, email, and notes.
