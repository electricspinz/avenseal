# SOP: Failed Appointment

## No-show

Offer one courtesy reschedule for a first no-show, subject to approved policy and appointment availability.

## Identity verification failure or notary refusal

Do not diagnose or override the determination. Escalate to the commissioned notary. Offer refund or one complimentary reschedule for identity failure; provide a full refund for notary refusal under the draft policy.

## Technical failure

Record a safe operational outcome. Offer refund or a complimentary reschedule under approved policy. BlueNotary platform diagnostics remain with BlueNotary.
