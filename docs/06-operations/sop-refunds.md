# SOP: Refunds

1. Verify appointment, payment, and organization scope through approved administrative tools.
2. Apply the attorney-approved refund policy; do not invent exceptions.
3. Escalate duplicate charges, billing errors, disputes, or unclear facts to finance authority.
4. Never issue a refund solely from an email request without trusted appointment/payment verification.
5. Record only safe audit facts; do not expose processor identifiers or payment details.

Refund execution and receipt/reconciliation operations require launch approval; see [pricing model](../05-sales/pricing-and-service-model.md).
