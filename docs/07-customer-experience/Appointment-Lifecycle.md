# Appointment Lifecycle

## Purpose

Provide a customer-facing conceptual lifecycle without redefining repository statuses.

**Status:** Current working standard
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-08

## Current conceptual stages

Request or booking → review and scheduling → payment when applicable → preparation and communications → provider-hosted session handoff → completion or follow-up.

The exact operational status model, reminders, calendar synchronization, and readiness logic are implementation-owned; see [Architecture Overview](../09-technology/Architecture-Overview.md).

## Customer language

Describe a pending review or next step honestly. Do not tell a customer that notarization is complete until the authoritative operational process supports that statement.
