# Customer Journey

## Purpose

Describe the intended end-to-end customer experience accurately.

**Status:** Current working standard
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-08

## Journey

1. **Discover:** A customer finds Avenseal and understands the service.
2. **Request or schedule:** The customer provides the information needed for an appointment.
3. **Pay when required:** Avenseal handles the trusted payment experience.
4. **Prepare:** The customer receives appointment communications and can use the Client Workspace.
5. **Verify and join:** Identity verification and the remote session are handled by the online notarization provider.
6. **Complete:** A commissioned notary completes the notarization, subject to applicable requirements.

## Experience standard

Every stage should say what happens next, avoid unsupported promises, and preserve privacy. Avenseal must not imply that it hosts the identity-verification or remote-session platform.
