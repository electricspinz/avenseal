# Support Standards

## Purpose

Set a calm, privacy-conscious baseline for customer support.

**Status:** Planned / working standard
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-08

## Service standard

Provide helpful, plain-language guidance during published office hours: Monday–Friday, 9:30 AM–5:30 PM ET. Use appointments@avenseal.com and (727) 433-8565 only in approved support surfaces.

## Escalation

Route identity-verification or remote-session questions to the appropriate provider process. Route legal questions to appropriate qualified guidance; do not provide legal advice. Escalate security or privacy incidents using [Incident Response](../08-operations/Incident-Response.md).

## To Be Finalized

Response-time objectives, escalation owners, after-hours process, and approved support macros.
