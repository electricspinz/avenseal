# Financial Operations Notes

## Launch scope

The launch charge is only the disclosed notarial fee. No separate technology, platform, or convenience fee applies. Payment amounts must be server-derived from trusted appointment/service data.

## Operating controls

- Reconcile paid, refunded, disputed, and failed payment records through approved finance workflows.
- Keep Stripe credentials and payment identifiers server-side.
- Use the approved refund policy; do not make manual exceptions without finance authority.
- Retain finance records according to attorney/accountant-approved requirements, not this note.

Refund execution, receipts, and reconciliation require production-launch approval; current product boundaries are documented in [Payments](../03-product/payments.md).
