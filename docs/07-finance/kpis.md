# Launch KPI Definitions

**All targets are provisional until real operating data exists.** Do not report a metric as achieved without an approved source and measurement period.

| KPI | Definition | Provisional target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Booking conversion | Completed booking requests ÷ eligible booking starts | `[Set after baseline]` |
| Appointment completion | Completed notarizations confirmed by approved evidence ÷ scheduled appointments | `[Set after baseline]` |
| Response time | Median time to first support response | `[Set after baseline]` |
| Customer satisfaction | Approved post-service satisfaction measure | `[Set after baseline]` |
| Refund rate | Refunded appointments ÷ paid appointments | `[Set after baseline]` |
| No-show rate | No-shows ÷ scheduled appointments | `[Set after baseline]` |
| Identity verification failure rate | Provider/notary-confirmed identity failures ÷ sessions attempted | `[Set after baseline]` |
| Appointment duration | Median confirmed session duration | `[Set after baseline]` |
| Review rating | Average verified public rating | `[Set after baseline]` |
| Repeat customer rate | Returning customers ÷ completed customers | `[Set after baseline]` |

Analytics/reporting is not yet an authoritative production source; see [roadmap](../00-overview/roadmap.md).
