# Daily Operations

## Purpose

Provide a launch-safe daily operating checklist.

**Status:** Planned / working checklist
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-08

## Daily checklist

- [ ] Review upcoming appointments and actionable operational status.
- [ ] Review payment, communication, calendar, and provider-handoff exceptions through approved admin surfaces.
- [ ] Check customer inquiries and support escalations.
- [ ] Confirm scheduled workers and queues are healthy through approved monitoring.
- [ ] Review any document-security or privacy incidents through the defined escalation path.
- [ ] Record material exceptions without copying sensitive data into informal channels.

## Guardrails

Do not use customer data in unapproved tools. Do not manually bypass authorization, payment, provider, or document-security controls.
