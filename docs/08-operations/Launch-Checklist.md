# Operations Launch Checklist

## Purpose

Coordinate operational readiness without replacing engineering, legal, or business approval.

**Status:** Planned / working checklist
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-08

## Before launch

- [ ] Named owner has approved production access and escalation responsibilities.
- [ ] Public contact information and hours are confirmed.
- [ ] Booking, payment, communications, calendar, and provider handoff are tested through approved non-production or controlled processes.
- [ ] Required legal and business disclosures are approved.
- [ ] Monitoring and incident contacts are confirmed.
- [ ] Google Business Profile is verified with accurate service-area settings.
- [ ] Staff know how to handle support, refunds, provider handoff, and security incidents.
