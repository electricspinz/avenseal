# Monthly Operations

## Purpose

Set a monthly review rhythm for launch operations.

**Status:** Planned / working checklist
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-08

## Monthly checklist

- [ ] Reconfirm public contact information, hours, and service-area wording.
- [ ] Review public-site metadata, sitemap, and Google Business Profile completeness.
- [ ] Review customer journey friction and support trends.
- [ ] Review approved operating controls, access ownership, and incident follow-ups.
- [ ] Reconcile roadmap decisions with product and engineering documentation.
