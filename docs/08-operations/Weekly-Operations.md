# Weekly Operations

## Purpose

Set a lightweight weekly operating rhythm.

**Status:** Planned / working checklist
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-08

## Weekly checklist

- [ ] Review appointment volume, exceptions, and customer-support themes.
- [ ] Review failed or suppressed communications through approved operational tools.
- [ ] Review calendar and provider-handoff exceptions.
- [ ] Review Google Business Profile messages and reviews under approved response rules.
- [ ] Check launch-content accuracy, broken public links, and business-information consistency.
- [ ] Assign owners for unresolved operational risks.
