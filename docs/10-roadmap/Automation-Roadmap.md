# Automation Roadmap

## Purpose

Describe automation direction without overstating current or future autonomy.

**Status:** Planned / working roadmap
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-08

## Current

The repository contains deterministic workflow, communication, reminder, readiness, and document-security foundations. These systems are product-owned and server-side; they are not a public scripting language.

## Planned

Improve observability, controlled retries, and operational safety only through approved product and engineering work. Human approval remains appropriate for consequential actions.

## Future vision

AI providers may advise, but should not directly execute privileged actions. User-created workflows, natural-language automation, marketplace integrations, and autonomous execution are not current capability.
