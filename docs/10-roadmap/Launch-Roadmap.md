# Launch Roadmap

## Purpose

Sequence the remaining business and public-readiness decisions needed for launch.

**Status:** Planned / working roadmap
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-08

## Current

Core customer workflows are represented in the repository. Public positioning must continue to distinguish Avenseal from the provider-hosted notarization session.

## Before launch

1. Finalize legal/business information and approved public policies.
2. Confirm production operational configuration and owner responsibilities.
3. Complete public-site metadata, crawl, asset, and Google Business Profile readiness.
4. Validate customer workflows using approved controlled procedures.
5. Establish support, incident, review, and monitoring cadence.

## To Be Finalized

Launch authority, dates, acceptance criteria, external approvals, and contingency plan.
