# Licensing Roadmap

## Purpose

Keep future licensing exploration clearly separate from current customer capability.

**Status:** Future vision
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-08

## Direction

Avenseal may eventually license parts of its platform to other notaries for use on their own websites. No current documentation should represent a white-label or partner product as available.

## Discovery before commitment

Validate tenant boundaries, brand ownership, support model, data responsibility, provider relationships, commercial terms, and legal implications before creating an offer.
