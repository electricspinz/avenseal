# Product Roadmap

## Purpose

Give a truthful high-level view of current product foundations and future direction.

**Status:** Planned / working roadmap
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-08

## Current / implemented foundations

The repository contains public discovery and booking surfaces, admin operations, appointment scheduling, payment workflows, communications, Google Calendar boundaries, Client Workspace access, document-security foundations, and operational/readiness workflows.

## Planned

Prioritize launch validation, reliable operations, accurate public content, and controlled improvement based on customer and staff evidence. Specific dates and feature commitments are **To Be Finalized**.

## Future vision

Provider-neutral expansion, licensed/white-label offerings, and additional automation are strategic possibilities—not current customer promises.
