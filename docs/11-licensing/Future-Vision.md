# Licensing Future Vision

## Purpose

Describe the directional opportunity while protecting accuracy today.

**Status:** Future vision
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-08

## Vision

Avenseal could become a trusted customer-facing operating layer that qualified notary businesses use on their own websites, while preserving clear responsibility for customer experience, providers, data, and professional services.

## Guardrails

Future vision is not product availability. It does not authorize partner claims, cross-tenant data access, autonomous provider actions, or changes to the current Avenseal business model.

## Success criteria

Any future initiative must demonstrate approved business demand, safe tenant isolation, clear operational ownership, and validated legal/commercial terms.
