## Purpose

Record the future-only licensing thesis for Avenseal.

**Status:** Future vision

## Future direction

Avenseal may eventually license a customer-facing appointment platform to other notaries for use on their own websites. This is not a current offering, customer promise, or implementation commitment.

Commercial model, tenant/data boundaries, support ownership, branding rights, provider responsibilities, implementation model, legal terms, and approval process are **To Be Finalized**.
