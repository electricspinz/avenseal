# White-Label Strategy

## Purpose

Frame the questions that must be answered before any future white-label offer.

**Status:** Future vision
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-08

## Principle

A future partner experience must not dilute trust, obscure accountable ownership, or weaken tenant and customer boundaries.

## Discovery areas

- Partner brand and domain ownership
- Organization and data isolation
- Customer communications and support responsibility
- Provider/session disclosure
- Payment and refund responsibility
- Configuration, onboarding, and offboarding
- Security, audit, and legal obligations

## Not current

No white-label UI, self-service partner onboarding, public licensing plan, or partner integration should be advertised as available.
